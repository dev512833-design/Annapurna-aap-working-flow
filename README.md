# Annapurna-aap-working-flow
Annapurna – Waste Food Management System

Save Food Today, Feed Someone Tomorrow.

Annapurna is a smart digital food redistribution platform designed to reduce avoidable food waste by connecting surplus food donors with nearby receivers, NGOs, volunteers, and animal welfare organizations.

The platform enables food donors to list surplus food with details such as food type, quantity, location, and pickup deadline. Nearby receivers and volunteers can discover available donations, request them, and track the donation journey from pickup to final distribution.

The system also includes animal food redistribution, receiver-only subscription plans, review and rating functionality, real-time notifications, location-based matching, analytics, and future AI-powered surplus prediction.

Key Features

- Surplus food listing and management
- Location-based donor–receiver matching
- Pickup deadline and request management
- End-to-end donation tracking
- Human and animal food redistribution
- Receiver-only subscription system
- Donor–receiver review and rating
- Real-time notifications
- Impact analytics
- AI-based surplus prediction
- GPS-based matching
- Route optimization
- QR-based tracking

Vision

To create a transparent, technology-driven ecosystem where surplus food reaches people and animals who need it instead of becoming waste.
